# Editorial Pre-Publish QA Checklist for Google Discover & News

- [ ] **High-Resolution Hero Image:** Featured image is >= 1200px wide (16:9 ratio) with `max-image-preview:large` enabled.
- [ ] **Compelling Non-Clickbait Title:** Engaging Arabic headline stating real news without deceptive ambiguity.
- [ ] **Bylined Verified Author:** Real journalist name linked to author profile page.
- [ ] **Immediate Value Opening:** Core development summarized in first 50 words.
- [ ] **Original Sourcing:** Clear attribution to primary government or news sources.
